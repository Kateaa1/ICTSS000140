The Menu

Real Milk. Real Cows. Really.

We got tired of oat milk. And almond milk. And "barista blend" anything-but-milk. So we did something wild we brought the cows to the coffee shop.

The Mooo Bar Cafe is a working micro-dairy and specialty coffee bar rolled into one. Every cup served here is made with milk drawn that morning (sometimes that hour) from one of our five hand-raised, ridiculously affectionate Jersey cows. You can watch it happen. You can help it happen. You can even give the milk-maker a scratch behind the ears while you wait.

No powders. No processing. No pretending. Just coffee, cream, and cows the way nature very clearly intended.
